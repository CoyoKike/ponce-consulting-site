How I work · V
Approach

I listen first

Every project starts with the people who'll use it: what they need, what worries them, what they're quietly working around. That conversation shapes the work more than any document, and it keeps the project pointed at what your team actually wants. Most of what I touch is sensitive data, so security is built in from day one: only the right people can see it, every change is recorded, and it never leaves your own systems. Then I build, show it early, and hand it over with clear instructions and tests. No black boxes. Your team owns what I leave behind.
