About us

Neighbors helping neighbors find help.

Vecina is a small Boston nonprofit. We keep a free, up-to-date map of community programs, in the languages our neighbors speak, and we build it so everyone can use it.

Our story

It started with a stack of flyers.

In 2019, a librarian and two community health workers compared notes. Each of them kept a folder of flyers, phone numbers and sticky notes to help people find food, a clinic or rent help. Half of the numbers were out of date, and almost none of it was in Haitian Creole, Chinese or Vietnamese.

They put the folders together into one shared list, then into a map. Today Vecina is run by a team of five and a group of volunteers who call every program on the map twice a year to check that it is still open.

What we do

  • 01

    Keep the map current

    Volunteers check every listing twice a year. Programs can send updates any time, and we confirm them by phone.

  • 02

    Speak your language

    The map works in six languages today. Community members review the wording so it sounds natural, not machine-made.

  • 03

    Build for everyone

    The site is built to meet WCAG 2.1 AA, so it works with a keyboard, a screen reader, a small phone or large text.

  • 04

    Train front-line staff

    We run short trainings for library, clinic and school staff on how to use the map with the people they serve.
