Our mission

For more than forty years we have built homes people can afford, helped families build savings and careers, and kept small businesses on our corners, so this neighborhood stays home for the people who made it.

“Every family we help stay in Cedar Hill keeps a little more of this neighborhood’s story alive.”

Marisol Vega · Executive Director

What we do

Affordable housing

Homes people can afford.

We build, own and manage 1,100 affordable apartments, and help first-time buyers get their keys.

  • Apartments and waitlists
  • First-time homebuyer classes
  • Foreclosure and eviction help
Learn more

Family prosperity

Steady work, growing savings.

One-on-one coaches help families find better jobs, file taxes for free, build credit and save for what matters.

  • Career coaching and job placement
  • Free tax preparation
  • Financial and credit coaching
Learn more

Small business

Keep our corners open.

Coaching, small loans and help with permits, marketing and leases for the shops that make Cedar Hill feel like home.

  • Business coaching in English and Spanish
  • Microloans up to $50,000
  • Storefront and lease support
Learn more

Community organizing

A neighborhood with a voice.

Residents lead campaigns for fair rents, safe streets and good jobs, and we train the next generation of leaders.

  • Tenant associations
  • Leadership training
  • Community meetings every month
Learn more
