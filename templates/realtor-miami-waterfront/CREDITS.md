## 3D scene
Casa Marea, the yacht, the palms, the skyline and the water are built in code (`js/hero.js`): the house is laid out from real modern-villa proportions, the palm fronds are drawn on a canvas, the water uses a live planar reflection, and the sky and its light are computed per frame for golden hour, blue hour and night.
