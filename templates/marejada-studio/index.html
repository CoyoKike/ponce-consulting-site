01 · Classes

Four classes. Every one coached.

No room holds more than fourteen people, so your coach sees your form on every set. Mix all four and your week is covered.

01

Strength

50 minAll levels

Barbells, kettlebells and dumbbells on a written plan that runs in six-week blocks, so you watch the weight go up.

02

Reformer

50 minAll levels

Springs, slow tempo, hard work. Builds the deep core and hips that hold you up when you lift and when you run.

03

Ride

45 minOpen

Low light and a playlist built for the climb. Every bike has its own screen with your watts, not the room's.

04

Mobility

40 minAll levels

Hips, spine and shoulders. The class that makes the other three feel better, and the one regulars never skip.
