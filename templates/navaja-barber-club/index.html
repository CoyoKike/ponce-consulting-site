01The shop

Four chairs on La Séptima, a cafecito on the counter and a straight razor stropped on leather every morning.

Ybor City was built by cigar rollers, and every one of them needed a barber. We kept the trade the way they did it: towels steamed hot, razors honed by hand, and time to talk while we work.

The cuts are new. The manners are not. Everyone leaves with a clean neckline, a straight edge and a splash of bay rum. If anything is off, we fix it in the same chair.

03The ritual

Forty-five minutes of quiet

01

Hot towel

Three steamed towels, one after another, to open the pores and soften the beard. This is where the week starts to slow down.

02

Lather

Pre-shave oil, then a warm lather whipped with a brush and worked in against the grain.

03

Two passes

One with the grain, one across it. A fresh blade for every client, skin held taut, a steady hand.

04

Cold towel & bay rum

A cold towel to close, an alcohol-free balm and the old smell: bay rum, the way the barbershops on this street always smelled.
