Services

Two desks, one visit.

We prepare your return and review your policies in the same appointment. When life changes, a baby, a house, a new business, we look at it from both sides.

  1. 01

    Personal returns

    W-2 income, Child Tax Credit, Earned Income Credit, childcare and tuition. We check the last three years for credits nobody claimed.

    Form 1040
  2. 02

    Self-employed & 1099

    Rideshare drivers, contractors, stylists and cleaners. Mileage, tools and phone deducted from your records, plus quarterly payments so April doesn't sting.

    Schedule C · SE
  3. 03

    Small business & bookkeeping

    LLCs, S corporations and partnerships. Monthly books, payroll and the annual return, done by the team that already knows your numbers.

    1120-S · 1065
  4. 04

    ITIN applications

    As a Certifying Acceptance Agent we verify your passport here, so you never have to mail the original to the IRS.

    Form W-7
  5. 05

    Amended & prior-year returns

    Missed a form, or skipped a year? We put the years back in order, one at a time, and tell you what to expect before anything is filed.

    1040-X
  6. 06

    Letters from the IRS

    Bring the envelope, unopened if you like. We explain the letter in your language, write the response and follow it until it's closed.

    CP2000 · CP14
  1. 01

    Auto

    We quote several carriers at once. The PIP and property damage Florida requires, plus the uninsured motorist coverage that really matters here.

    PIP · PD · UM
  2. 02

    Home, condo & renters

    Wind, flood and the hurricane deductible explained in real dollars. We order the wind mitigation inspection that can lower your premium.

    HO-3 · HO-6 · HO-4
  3. 03

    Life

    Term coverage sized to the mortgage and the kids' school years. Short questions, one meeting, and the policy explained to whoever you name.

    Term · Final expense
  4. 04

    Marketplace health

    Enrollment runs November 1 to January 15. We estimate your premium credit with your tax return in hand, so the 1095-A holds no surprises.

    ACA · 1095-A
  5. 05

    Commercial

    General liability, commercial auto and workers' comp for contractors, food trucks and storefronts. Certificates issued the same day.

    GL · WC · BOP

Key dates

The tax year, date by date.

The 2027 season, for your 2026 return. We text you before each one that applies to you.

  1. 15 Jan

    Marketplace enrollment ends

    Last day to pick a 2027 health plan. The final 2026 estimated payment is also due.

  2. 1 Feb

    W-2s and 1099-NECs arrive

    Employers have until this Monday to send them. Missing one? Tell us.

  3. 15 Mar

    S corps & partnerships

    Forms 1120-S and 1065 are due, or an extension.

  4. 15 Apr

    Personal returns

    2026 returns, extensions and the first 2027 estimated payment are due.

  5. 1 Jun

    Hurricane season begins

    Review your policy first: once a storm is on the way, insurers stop writing new coverage.

  6. 15 Oct

    Extended returns

    Final day for personal returns on extension.

  7. 1 Nov

    Marketplace opens

    Open enrollment for 2028 health coverage starts.
