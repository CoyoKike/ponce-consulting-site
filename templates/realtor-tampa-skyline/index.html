03 — Condo guide

Buying a condo in Florida changed in 2022.

Since the state's SB 4-D law, buildings of three storeys or more need milestone inspections and a structural reserve study. It changes dues, special assessments and financing. I check it before you sign.

If you are buying

  1. HOA documents, read for youBudget, reserves, board minutes and pending litigation, summarized in plain language within three days.
  2. Milestone inspection and reservesWhere the building stands on its inspection schedule, and what assessments may be coming.
  3. Rental and pet rulesMinimum lease terms, short-term rentals, pet limits: before you fall for the view.
  4. Parking, storage and insuranceAssigned or valet parking, storage units, and an HO-6 insurance quote from day one.
