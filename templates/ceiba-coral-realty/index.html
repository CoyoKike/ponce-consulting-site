Services

Four ways to move.

  1. 01

    Buying

    We send you homes before they hit the portals, read every inspection report with you, and negotiate like it is our own mortgage.

    • Evening and weekend showings
    • Lenders who work in both languages
    • Flood zone and insurance review
  2. 02

    Selling

    A price built from the sales on your own street, professional photos and video, and a list of buyers already looking in your neighborhood.

    • Pricing study in 48 hours
    • Staging and prep crew
    • Listing written in English and Spanish
  3. 03

    Relocation & first homes

    Moving from Bogotá, Madrid or New York? Buying for the first time? We walk you through closing, Florida down payment programs and schools, one step at a time.

    • Video tours from any country
    • Schools and commute guide
    • Foreign buyers and ITIN loans
  4. 04

    Pre-construction

    We read the developer contract before you sign: deposit schedule, delivery dates and what is actually included.

    • Towers in Brickell, Edgewater and Coconut Grove
    • Price per square foot compared
    • With you until the walkthrough
