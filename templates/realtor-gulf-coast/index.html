Places we know

Six Gulf towns, six different paces.

  • Naples & Port Royal

    Shaded streets within walking distance of Fifth Avenue and the pier. Port Royal adds canal homes with direct Gulf access.

    Good for Walking everywhere, boating

  • Marco Island

    An island of canals, fine-sand beach and Gulf-front condos. Quieter than Naples and twenty minutes away.

    Good for Condos, fishing, long seasons

  • Siesta Key

    Quartz sand that stays cool, old cottages and new homes on pilings. Rental rules change from one zone to the next.

    Good for Beach houses that rent

  • Sarasota & Bird Key

    Opera, museums and restaurants downtown; bayfront homes on Bird Key and Lido, one bridge away.

    Good for Culture and water, close together

  • Lakewood Ranch

    Golf, trails and newer homes inland, outside the coastal evacuation zones and with gentler insurance.

    Good for Golf and year-round club life

  • Venice & Manasota Key

    A town of palm-lined avenues and a long, narrow key where 1960s beach houses still stand.

    Good for A simpler life by the water

Buying from afar

Buy from home. Arrive with the keys.

Many of our buyers first walk into their home on moving day. Here is how we make that work without surprises.

  1. 01

    An unhurried call

    We talk about how you want to live: how many months a year, who comes to visit, whether you want to rent it while you are away.

  2. 02

    Live video tours

    We walk every home with you on screen, open the closets, step onto the balcony and show you what the listing camera left out.

  3. 03

    Storms, elevation, insurance

    Before you offer we read the elevation certificate, the flood zone, the wind mitigation report and a real insurance quote.

  4. 04

    A remote closing

    E-signatures, a mobile notary wherever you are, and we meet the utilities, the keys and the air conditioning technician.

Services

For buying, for selling.

  • A second home from afar

    We search, tour and negotiate for you, with live video and written notes on every home.

  • Hurricane and insurance review

    Elevation, roof age, impact windows, generator and the true cost of insurance, before you sign.

  • Seasonal rental rules

    Every association sets its own minimums: 30 days, 90 days or none. We tell you before you fall in love.

  • Golf and club memberships

    We explain initiation fees, waitlists and what each membership really includes.

  • Downsizing, gently

    We plan the sale and the next purchase together, with movers, donations and estate sales lined up.

  • Estate and trust sales

    We work with trustees and families who live far away: clean-out, repairs and photos without anyone having to fly in.

  • Timing the season

    Northern buyers arrive between January and April. We prepare the house in the fall so it is ready when they are.

  • Pricing with evidence

    A written valuation built on comparable sales, elevation and roof condition, not a number pulled from the air.
