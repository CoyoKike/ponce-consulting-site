01 The way we clean

Top to bottom, dry to wet, room by room. The same order every visit, so nothing gets skipped and you always know who is at your door.

We started cleaning condos on the beach side of Fort Lauderdale, where salt air leaves a film on every slider and the AC pulls dust into every vent. We still clean that way: vents and tracks first, glass before floors, and a checklist taped inside the caddy so nothing depends on memory.

Every client gets a set team of two. They learn where the dog hides, which cabinet sticks and how you like the towels folded. If someone is out sick, we tell you the night before and send someone who has read your notes.

03 How it works

Four steps, and the same faces.

  1. 01

    Book

    Pick a day online or text us. Tell us about pets, parking, the gate code and anything that needs a gentle hand.

  2. 02

    Your team arrives

    Two people you will get to know, on time, with supplies and equipment. If you are out, they use your lockbox or the front desk.

  3. 03

    Room by room

    A written checklist for each room. High to low, dry work before wet, and the floors last on the way out the door.

  4. 04

    Walkthrough and photos

    Home? Walk through it together. Away? A photo of every room lands on your phone before they lock up. Anything we missed, we come back for within 24 hours.

09 Good to know

Before the first visit.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most clients leave a key with the front desk or give us a lockbox code. We keep keys tagged with a number, never with your name or address.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Plant-based cleaners, a HEPA vacuum, mops and color-coded microfiber, so bathroom cloths never touch your kitchen. If you prefer your own products, leave them out and we will use them.

My building asks for a certificate of insurance.

Send us the management office's email and any wording they need. We usually send the certificate the same day, and we keep it on file for every visit after.

What if something gets missed?

Tell us within 24 hours. The same team comes back to fix it, at no charge.

We have pets. Is that OK?

Of course. Tell us their names, where they like to hide and which doors should stay closed. We note it on your checklist so every visit goes the same way.
