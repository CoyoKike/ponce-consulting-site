The house

A 1926 house on Giralda with high ceilings, north light and six chairs facing arched windows.

Consultation first

We talk about your routine, your photos and how much time you give your hair in the morning. If what you ask for won't suit your hair, we will tell you.

Color made for the sun

Formulas toned so they don't turn brassy at the beach, and a gloss included with every color service.

Your hair, your texture

Curls are cut dry, curl by curl. Straight hair is cut with a slide technique so it falls into place on its own.
