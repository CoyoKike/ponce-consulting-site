## 3D
The hero engine (crankshaft, rods, pistons, liners, camshaft, valves, springs, flywheel and timing belt) is modeled in code in `js/engine.js`, with real slider-crank motion and a 1-3-4-2 firing order. Reflections use three.js RoomEnvironment. No downloaded models.
