The studio

Clean before beautiful.

A white room full of morning light, eight teak pedicure chairs and no smell of acetone. This is what a spa feels like when nobody is in a hurry.

  • Medical autoclave

    Every steel tool is sterilized and opened from its sealed pouch in front of you.

  • Single-use files

    Files, buffers and pumice are new for every guest. You take them home.

  • Basins without jets

    Copper basins with disposable liners and no hidden pipes.

  • 10-free polish

    No formaldehyde, toluene or DBP. Extraction vents at every table.
