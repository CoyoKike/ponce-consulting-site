    Neighborhoods

    Five neighborhoods, told by people who live in them.

    From Palmetto traffic to which school each block is zoned for: what never makes the listing.

    1. Hialeah

      The city that never stopped speaking Spanish. Concrete-block houses from the 50s and 60s, duplexes that pay for themselves and a ventanita on every corner. Good for investors and first homes alike.

      Houses from the $500s · duplexes from the $700s

    2. Doral

      Golf courses, new condos in Downtown Doral and some of the most requested schools in the west. Many families from Venezuela and Colombia land here first.

      Condos from the $400s · houses from $1M

    3. Miami Lakes

      A town planned around lakes, with curving streets, mature trees and Main Street within walking distance. Lakefront houses tend to sell before a sign goes up.

      Houses from the $800s · lakefront from $1.2M

    4. Miami Springs

      1920s Pueblo and Mission-style houses under huge oaks, next to the airport but with a small-town soul. Little inventory, a lot of loyalty.

      Houses from the $650s

    5. Westchester

      Single-story houses with big yards, close to FIU and Tropical Park. Where many families buy a first house and stay thirty years.

      Houses from the $550s
