## 3D map
The hero map of west Miami-Dade is built entirely in code with three.js: tiles, houses, towers, palms, canals, the expressway, the airport and the agent pins. No downloaded models or textures; the ground map is painted on a canvas at load time. The neighborhood shapes are simplified and not to scale.
