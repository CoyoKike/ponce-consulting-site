What we do

Everything outside the front door, handled.

Most of our clients start with weekly lawn care and add the rest as the yard comes together. One crew lead, one number to text.

  1. 01

    Weekly lawn care

    Mowed at the right height for your grass, string-trimmed along every bed and walk, edges cut sharp and clippings blown off the drive. Weekly from April through October, every other week once it cools off.

    St. Augustine · Zoysia · Bahia

  2. 02

    Florida-friendly landscaping

    Beds designed around what actually grows here: coontie, muhly grass, firebush, Simpson's stopper and sabal palms, placed for your sun, your shade and how much you want to water.

    Natives · Pollinators · Low water

  3. 03

    Palm trimming

    Sabal, queen, foxtail and date palms cleaned up before storm season. We take the brown fronds and seed stalks and leave the green crown full. No hurricane cuts.

    Fronds · Seed pods · Cleanup

  4. 04

    Mulch & pine straw

    Pine bark mulch or long-needle pine straw, laid two to three inches deep, pulled back from trunks and stems, and topped up twice a year so the beds always look fresh.

    Pine bark · Pine straw · Twice a year

  5. 05

    Irrigation tune-ups

    Zone-by-zone checks, heads raised and re-aimed, drip lines in the beds, a working rain sensor, and a controller set to your watering days: two a week in summer, one in winter, never between 10 and 4.

    Drip · Rain sensor · Watering days

  6. 06

    Pavers, travertine & stone

    Patios, walks and pool decks on a compacted base with polymeric sand, plus natural stone borders and river rock where grass won't grow.

    Pavers · Travertine · Natural stone

  7. 07

    Landscape lighting

    Low-voltage warm white LED: path lights along the walk, uplights under the palms, a few soft washes on the house. On a timer, with the wiring buried.

    2700K LED · Uplights · Timers

  8. 08

    Seasonal color

    Pentas, salvia and bulbine through the heat, then petunias, snapdragons and dianthus from November to March. Swapped out before they start to look tired.

    Summer · Winter · Containers

How it works

From a walk around the yard to every Thursday after.

  1. 01

    We walk the yard

    A crew lead comes out, walks the property with you, runs each irrigation zone and looks at the soil. No charge.

  2. 02

    You get a written plan

    A simple drawing, a plant list and a line-by-line price within two business days, so you know exactly what goes in the ground.

  3. 03

    Install day

    Trailer parked on the street, not your grass. Beds cut, plants in, stone set, and everything blown clean before we pull out.

  4. 04

    Same crew, same day

    Weekly visits on a set day. If something looks off, text a photo to your crew lead and it's handled on the next visit.
