Our mission

To keep Cedar Hill home for the families who built it, with homes they can afford, work that pays and shops that stay open.

About us

Started by twelve tenants on Linden Street. Still run by the neighborhood.

Since 1981 we have kept Cedar Hill home for the families who built it. Today we are 48 staff and 2,000 members. Meet the people behind the work and read how it all began.
