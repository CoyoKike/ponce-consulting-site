Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in during office hours and someone will help you the same day.

Does it cost anything?

Never. Every Raíces program is free for Cedar Hill residents.

Can I get help in Spanish?

Yes. Most of our staff speak Spanish, and we can arrange Haitian Creole, Portuguese and Vietnamese.

How do waitlists work?

Fill out one form and we add you to every building you qualify for. We call you when a home opens.
